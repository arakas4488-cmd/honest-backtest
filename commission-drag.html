How it is calculated

  • Risk $ = shares × stop. Reward $ = shares × take-profit.
  • Round-trip cost = 2 × max(shares × commission, minimum) + 2 × shares × slippage.
  • Net win = reward − cost. Net loss = risk + cost.
  • Breakeven win rate = net loss ÷ (net win + net loss).
  • Expectancy = win rate × net win − (1 − win rate) × net loss, shown in dollars and as a multiple of the trade's nominal risk (R).

Where the idea comes from: in my own trading bot, the round-trip commission was roughly the same size as the stop/target width, and an honest backtest with commission included turned a plausible-looking expectancy into a clearly negative one. This tool lets you check your own numbers in ten seconds.
