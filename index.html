Case Study 02 · My own bot

Auditing the system I trade with

Before offering this to anyone else, I audited my own live options/equity bot the same way.

Expected +0.254R on paper → −1.366R once real round-trip commission was counted

Plus three real production bugs found in the live git history: stop orders that expired overnight (TIF=DAY), stop/target levels computed from the signal price instead of the actual fill, and a hardcoded exchange assumption that silently broke risk checks for one symbol.
